## Interpretation

- The catalog deliberately includes commercial, membership, nonprofit, service, public-sector, and production functions in **one list**.
- **Manage Campaigns** and **Manage Fundraising Campaigns** can share common methods without being forced into identical goals. Overlap is documented and refined during discovery.
- **Manage Volunteers** is not automatically equivalent to **Manage Employment**; both may reuse assignment, participation, and scheduling knowledge.
- **Plan Events / Conduct Events** refers to business events such as conferences or fundraising dinners, not the technical concept of a software event.
- **Work** can organize any undertaking at arbitrary depth; project, task, activity, case, and operation are business classifications, not mandatory structural boundaries.
- Domain terminology is maintained in domain views, without multiplying canonical definitions solely because names differ.

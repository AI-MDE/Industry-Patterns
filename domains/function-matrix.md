## Terminology examples (labels, not copied function definitions)

| Donor Development | Cultivate Donors and Supporters; Manage Party Relationships | Donations are not sales |

## Gap review

Early gaps to validate in domain interviews: clinical care and treatment, underwriting and claims adjudication, financial account servicing, transportation ticketing, education assessment, government permitting, nonprofit beneficiary eligibility, and regulated sector obligations. These may require **additional canonical functions** after review. Do not force distinct activities into an over-general existing label solely to avoid adding entries.

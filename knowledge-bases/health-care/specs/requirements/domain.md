# Health Care Domain

## Purpose

Support safe, coordinated, traceable health-care administration and delivery from Patient access through clinical service and financial resolution.

## Scope and boundaries

Included: Patient administration; scheduling; Encounters; longitudinal coordination; clinical evidence; Service Requests and Delivery; consent; privacy; audit; Coverage; Authorization; Claims; adjudication.

Excluded until specialized: detailed medication administration; laboratory and imaging subsystems; pharmacy dispensing; inpatient bed operations; public-health surveillance; research operations; jurisdiction-specific coding, messaging, privacy, and reimbursement profiles.

## Core terminology

- **Appointment:** planned allocation of time and resources.
- **Encounter:** actual bounded care interaction.
- **Episode of Care:** period coordinating related care.
- **Medical Case:** managed body of work requiring evidence and resolution.
- **Care Plan:** goals and planned activities.
- **Service Request:** clinical or administrative request for a service.
- **Service Delivery:** evidence that a service was performed.
- **Authorization:** permission from a payer or organization; it does not replace Consent.
- **Consent:** Patient or representative decision for a defined purpose and scope.
- **Claim:** financial request to a Payer; it is not the clinical record.

# Authorized Service Must Match Conditions

## Statement

A service performed under an Authorization must match its approved Patient, service, quantity, Provider, Location, and effective period.

## Applies To

Authorization and Service Delivery.

## Condition

Authorization is used as financial or organizational authority.

## Constraint / Result

Validate every applicable condition before delivery or Claim submission.

## Exceptions

Approved amendment or documented emergency policy.

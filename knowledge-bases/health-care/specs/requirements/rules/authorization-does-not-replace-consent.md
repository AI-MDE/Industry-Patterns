# Authorization Does Not Replace Consent

## Statement

Payer or organizational Authorization does not substitute for Patient Consent or clinical authority.

## Applies To

Service authorization and delivery.

## Condition

An Authorization exists.

## Constraint / Result

Evaluate consent and clinical authority independently.

## Exceptions

Only where governing law explicitly provides authority.

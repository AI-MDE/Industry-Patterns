# Observation Requires Subject Time Source

## Statement

An Observation must identify its subject, observed time or period, code, value or explicit absence reason, status, and source.

## Applies To

Observation.

## Condition

Before an Observation becomes preliminary or final.

## Constraint / Result

Require complete semantic and provenance context.

## Exceptions

None.

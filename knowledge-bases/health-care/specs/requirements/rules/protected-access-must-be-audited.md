# Protected Access Must Be Audited

## Statement

Access to protected health information requiring audit must produce immutable evidence.

## Applies To

Views, changes, disclosures, exports, and emergency access.

## Condition

Protected information is accessed.

## Constraint / Result

Record actor, subject, purpose, action, time, source, and outcome.

## Exceptions

Only events explicitly excluded by governing policy.

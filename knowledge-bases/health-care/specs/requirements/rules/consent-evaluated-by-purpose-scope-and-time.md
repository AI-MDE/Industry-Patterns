# Consent Evaluated By Purpose Scope And Time

## Statement

Consent is valid only for its recorded decision, purpose, data or service scope, actor scope, and effective period.

## Applies To

Care, disclosure, research, and delegated access.

## Condition

An operation depends on Consent.

## Constraint / Result

Permit only when all dimensions match an active consent.

## Exceptions

Legal authority independent of consent must be cited and audited.

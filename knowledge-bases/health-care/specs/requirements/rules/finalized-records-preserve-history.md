# Finalized Records Preserve History

## Statement

Finalized clinical evidence is corrected by amendment or supersession, not destructive overwrite.

## Applies To

Clinical Notes, Observations, Diagnoses, Procedures, and Service Delivery evidence.

## Condition

A finalized record needs correction.

## Constraint / Result

Create a new version linked to the prior version with author, time, and reason.

## Exceptions

None.

# Claim Totals Must Reconcile

## Statement

Claim totals must equal the sum of valid Claim Lines and declared adjustments under consistent currency and rounding rules.

## Applies To

Claim validation, correction, and adjudication.

## Condition

Before submission or closure.

## Constraint / Result

Reconcile totals or block the transition.

## Exceptions

Explicit payer rounding rules.

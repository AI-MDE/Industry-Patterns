# Service Delivery Requires Traceability

## Statement

Service Delivery must identify the Patient, service, performer, time, Location or channel, governing authority, and result evidence.

## Applies To

Service Delivery.

## Condition

Before completion.

## Constraint / Result

Block completion until required traceability is present.

## Exceptions

Emergency basis is allowed only when explicitly recorded and later reviewed.

# Coverage Must Be Effective On Service Date

## Statement

Coverage used for a Claim or Authorization must be effective for the Patient on the relevant service date.

## Applies To

Coverage verification, Authorization, and Claim.

## Condition

Coverage is selected.

## Constraint / Result

Confirm effective dates, member identity, payer, and plan.

## Exceptions

Retroactive coverage remains pending until verified.

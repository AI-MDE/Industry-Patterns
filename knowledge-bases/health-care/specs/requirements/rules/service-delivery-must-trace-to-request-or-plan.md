# Service Delivery Must Trace To Request Or Plan

## Statement

Service Delivery must trace to a valid Service Request, Care Plan activity, standing authority, or documented emergency basis.

## Applies To

Service Delivery.

## Condition

Before completion.

## Constraint / Result

Require the governing source and its Patient, service, performer, time, and authority.

## Exceptions

Emergency basis must be explicitly recorded.

# Patient

## Purpose

Receives care and manages personal participation, preferences, consent, appointments, and authorized access.

## Responsibilities

Provide accurate information; participate in care decisions; manage appointments and consent.

## Permissions / Constraints

View permitted personal information; request appointments; provide or revoke consent; communicate with the care team.

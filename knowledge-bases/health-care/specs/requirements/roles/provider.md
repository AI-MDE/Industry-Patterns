# Provider

## Purpose

Assesses, orders, performs, documents, interprets, or coordinates care.

## Responsibilities

Deliver care within active credentials and scope; preserve accurate clinical evidence.

## Permissions / Constraints

Access necessary Patient information and perform authorized clinical operations.

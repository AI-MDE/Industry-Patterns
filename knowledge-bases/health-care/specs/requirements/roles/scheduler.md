# Scheduler

## Purpose

Manages availability and appointments for Patients, Providers, services, and Locations.

## Responsibilities

Create and change appointments while respecting capacity, priority, and access rules.

## Permissions / Constraints

Maintain schedules and appointment logistics; no implied access to unrelated clinical detail.

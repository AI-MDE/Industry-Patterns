# Care Coordinator

## Purpose

Coordinates services, participants, transitions, and follow-up across an Episode or Case.

## Responsibilities

Maintain the care plan, resolve coordination gaps, and track progress toward outcomes.

## Permissions / Constraints

Coordinate authorized services and view information required for the assigned care context.

# Privacy Officer

## Purpose

Governs access, disclosure, consent, restrictions, and audit review.

## Responsibilities

Define controls, investigate exceptions, and verify privacy evidence.

## Permissions / Constraints

Review protected audit and policy evidence under heightened authorization.

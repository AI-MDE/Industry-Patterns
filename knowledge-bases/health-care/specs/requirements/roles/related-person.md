# Related Person

## Purpose

Acts as guardian, caregiver, family contact, or authorized representative for a Patient.

## Responsibilities

Support the Patient within the authority granted by relationship, law, and consent.

## Permissions / Constraints

Access or act only within recorded authority, consent, and purpose.

# Billing Specialist

## Purpose

Converts documented service delivery into valid charges and claims.

## Responsibilities

Validate coding, coverage, authorization, claim composition, and reconciliation.

## Permissions / Constraints

Access the minimum clinical and financial information needed for billing.

# Subjects

The canonical model, by **subject**: an area of reusable business knowledge with one coherent responsibility. Industries are [industry patterns](../../catalog.md): they select and explain subjects, and name what an application typically adds. Each subject holds ABEs; each ABE, its entities.

| [Classification](classification/README.md) | Governed vocabularies and categories, and their assignment to anything. | 1 | 3 |

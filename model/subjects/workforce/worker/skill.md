# Skill

Subject: [Workforce](../README.md). ABE: [Worker](README.md).

Also known as: Skill (Professional Services).

## Definition and detail

Area of technical expertise, flagged when certification is required to exercise it. Binds the **Capability** role.

### In industries

- **Skill** (Professional Services): capability, certification, technology, domain, language, or credential; Professionals have Skills and Projects require them.

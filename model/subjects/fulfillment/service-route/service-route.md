# Service Route

Subject: [Fulfillment and Logistics](../README.md). ABE: [Service Route](README.md).

## Definition and detail

A planned sequence of geographically dispersed service stops for a resource or crew during an operational period. Distinct from the real execution and proof of service.

## Relationships

A route has ordered Route Stops, and uses existing geographic location, assignment, workforce and resource definitions. Stop execution refers to canonical Service Event and work outcomes.

## Rules

Replanning records route revisions. Sequencing respects arrival windows, capacity, travel feasibility, resource qualifications, and priority policies.

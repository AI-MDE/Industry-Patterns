# Appointment Policy

Subject: [Scheduling and Reservations](../README.md). ABE: [Appointment](README.md).

## Definition and detail

Configurable business restrictions governing time windows, notice, deposits, cancellations, rescheduling, waitlists, and no-show charges for appointment bookings.

## Rules

A policy has effective dates and an explicit precedence; preserve the version applied at booking. Charges and refunds reference canonical Finance and Agreement concepts, rather than creating another payment model.

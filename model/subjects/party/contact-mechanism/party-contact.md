# Party Contact

Subject: [Parties and Roles](../README.md). ABE: [Contact Mechanism](README.md).

Also known as: Contact (Equipment Service), Patient Contact (Health Care).

## Definition and detail

Assignment of a Contact Mechanism to a Party for a stated purpose.

Logical attributes: Party Contact Identifier; Purpose Type; Primary Indicator; Effective From; Effective Through.

Examples of purpose: billing, shipping, legal, service, emergency, personal, or work.

### In industries

- **Contact** (Equipment Service): Person representing a Customer in a capacity such as site manager, operator, or procurement. Specializes Party Contact. One Contact is primary per Customer.
- **Patient Contact** (Health Care): A purpose-specific contact mechanism or address for a Patient. Attributes: Patient Contact Identifier; Purpose; Preferred Indicator; Confidential Indicator; Effective From; Effective Through.

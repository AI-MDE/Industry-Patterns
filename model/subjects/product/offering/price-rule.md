# Price Rule

Subject: [Products, Offerings, and Prices](../README.md). ABE: [Offering](README.md).

Also known as: Rating Rule (Telecommunications), Fare Rule or Rate Rule (Travel).

## Definition and detail

A governed rule converting usage, subscription, time, destination, quality, allowance, or event conditions into charge amounts.

Logical attributes: Rating Rule Identifier; Rule Type; Version; Effective From; Effective Through; Unit; Rate; Currency; Condition; Priority.

### In industries

- **Fare Rule or Rate Rule** (Travel): A condition governing eligibility, changes, cancellation, refund, advance purchase, stay, occupancy, baggage, or another commercial constraint. Attributes: Rule Identifier; Rule Type; Rule Status; Condition; Result; Penalty; Effective From; Effective Through.

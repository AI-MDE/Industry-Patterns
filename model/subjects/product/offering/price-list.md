# Price List

Subject: [Products, Offerings, and Prices](../README.md). ABE: [Offering](README.md).

Also known as: Rate Card (Crane Rental), Pricing Plan (Financial Services).

## Definition and detail

An effective-dated set of rental, labor, transport, overtime, standby, fuel, mobilization, permit, and other rates.

Logical attributes: Rate Card Identifier; Rate Card Name; Version; Currency; Territory; Customer Segment; Effective From; Effective Through; Status.

### In industries

- **Pricing Plan** (Financial Services): A governed definition of rates, fees, waivers, tiers, and calculation methods. Attributes: Pricing Plan Identifier; Plan Name; Version; Currency; Status; Effective From; Effective Through; Calculation Rule Reference.

# Service Request

Domain: [Service Management](../README.md). ABE: [Service Request](README.md).

Specializes: [Request](../../request-and-fulfillment/request/request.md). Inherit its meaning; apply the refinements below in this context.

## Definition and detail

An on-demand expression of need for a Service.

Typical examples: equipment breakdown, repair request, urgent treatment request, or ad-hoc room reservation.

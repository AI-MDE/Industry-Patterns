# Clinical Assertion Requires Provenance

## Statement

Every clinical assertion must identify its author or source, time, context, status, and supporting evidence as applicable.

## Applies To

Condition, Diagnosis, Observation, Procedure, and Clinical Note.

## Condition

A clinical assertion is recorded or imported.

## Constraint / Result

Retain provenance and distinguish source assertion from local verification.

## Exceptions

None.

## Canonical entities

- [Condition](../clinical-note/condition.md)

# Care Plan Must Identify Accountable Provider

## Statement

An active Care Plan must identify an accountable Provider or Care Coordinator.

## Applies To

Care Plan activation and review.

## Condition

Before activation.

## Constraint / Result

Require accountable role and review schedule.

## Exceptions

None.

## Canonical entities

- [Care Plan](../service-request/care-plan.md)

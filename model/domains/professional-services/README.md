# Professional Services

Professional Services is an industry profile for organizations that sell and deliver expert work through engagements, matters, projects, assignments, deliverables, and professional billing arrangements.

The profile deliberately reuses shared business domains instead of redefining enterprise administration.

## Uses shared domains

### Common

- Party — clients and other participants are parties playing roles.
- Agreement — service agreements inherit common agreement meaning.
- Classification — reusable categories and classifications.
- Measurement — quantities and measures used by delivery and billing.

### Administration

- Organization — practice organization, departments, positions, and reporting structure.
- Human Resources — workers, jobs, skills, competencies, and qualifications.
- Accounting — expenses, invoices, receivables, payments, and customer accounts.
- Facilities — offices and workplaces where relevant.

### Other reusable domains

- Work Management — project, task, work effort, approval, and time recording.
- Resource Management — assignments, capacity, and resource planning.
- Product and Service — service offerings.
- Scheduling — availability and assignment constraints when required.

## Professional Services concepts

The industry adds or specializes concepts that are distinctive to professional-service delivery:

- [Engagement](project/engagement.md)
- [Matter](project/matter.md)
- [Practice Area](project/practice-area.md)
- [Service Offering](project/service-offering.md)
- [Project Assignment](project/project-assignment.md) — professional-service specialization of assignment.
- [Deliverable](project/deliverable.md)
- [Billing Event](project/billing-event.md)
- [Billing Rate](project/billing-rate.md) — professional-service pricing specialization when required.

## Profile principle

A Professional Services application should compose these shared domains and add only the professional-service refinements it needs. Generic concepts such as Client, Professional, Skill, Project, Task, Time Entry, Expense, Approval, Invoice, Payment, Client Account, and Resource Plan are not owned by this industry profile.

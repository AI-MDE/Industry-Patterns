# Accounting

Reusable accounting and commercial administration.

Candidate concepts include:

- Account
- Chart of Accounts
- Accounting Period
- Journal
- Journal Entry
- Ledger
- Receivable
- Payable
- Invoice
- Invoice Line
- Expense
- Payment
- Customer Account
- Supplier Account

Accounting is distinct from the Finance domain. Accounting records and settles business transactions; Finance manages financial resources, planning, funding, treasury, investment, and related decisions.

# Route Stop

Domain: [Scheduling](../README.md). ABE: [Service Route](README.md).

## Definition

A sequenced planned visit to a service location on a route, with a service time window, expected duration, and optional status and proof-of-service references.

## Relationships

Belongs to a Service Route, optionally references Service Site, Service Request, Service Plan, and actual Service Event. The planned stop is not the actual completed visit.
